## Known errors and traps in the sources (do not teach the errors)

- **Watch out:** - The slide's "flip" **reverses the order** (`0001` → `1000`). C's `~` inverts every bit. Decoding a frame never inverts. - Baud counts bits, not bytes. At 8N1 one byte costs 10 bits, so 9600 baud carries 960 bytes/s, not 1200.
- **Watch out:** - `UDR0` is one name for two buffers. Writing `UDR0 = x;` fills the transmit buffer. Reading `x = UDR0;` gives the last received byte, not what you wrote. - Keep D0 and D1 free in UART programs. The USB adapter also uses them for flashing. - "Pin 2" and "Pin 3...
