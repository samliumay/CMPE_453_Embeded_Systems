## Known errors and traps in the sources (do not teach the errors)

- **Watch out:** - MOSI goes to MOSI and MISO to MISO. They are not crossed like UART TX/RX. - You must set the output pins in `DDRB` yourself. A master with `DDB3` and `DDB5` at 0 sends nothing. - D13 is also the on-board LED. It flickers with the clock, that is normal.
- **Watch out:** Full duplex does not mean either side can start. Only the master makes clock pulses, so only the master starts a transfer.
- **Watch out:** - Slide 11 says `SPI2X` "reduces the frequency divider from 4x to 2x". It halves **every** divider: 4 → 2, 16 → 8, 64 → 32, 128 → 64. - `CPHA` = 0 does not mean "sample on the rising edge". It means the leading edge, which rises or falls depending on `CPOL`.
- **Two bugs in the slide's master init:** 1. **`SPIE` without an interrupt routine.** No `ISR(SPI_STC_vect)` exists, so after the first transfer the CPU jumps to the default handler and **the program restarts**.
