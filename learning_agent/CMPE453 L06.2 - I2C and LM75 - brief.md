## Known errors and traps in the sources (do not teach the errors)

- **Watch out:** - The slide says "Eight bit". It means the eighth bit of the address byte (bit 0, sent last). - Datasheets give addresses as 7-bit (`0x48`) or already shifted (`0x90`). Shifting twice, or not at all, is the most common reason why a device does not answer.
- **Watch out:** A NACK is not always an error. The master sends a NACK on purpose after the last byte it wants to read.
- **Watch out:** `TWINT` is cleared by writing a **1** to it, not a 0. The 1 means "go". While `TWINT` is 1, SCL is held low and the bus waits.
- **Problems in the slide program:** - **The bit rate is for an 8 MHz chip** (the program comes from Williams' book). On the 16 MHz Uno `TWBR = 32` gives 200 kHz, not 100 kHz.
